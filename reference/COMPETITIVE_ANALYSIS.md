## Direct Competitors (other chains)

### Pump.fun (Solana)
- **Weekly Revenue**: $18.6M (per DefiLlama)
- **Custom Pairs Feature**: Launched October 9, 2026 (eliminates one of vibe/vibe's clearest differentiators)
- **Bonding Curve**: Constant-product x·y=k (NOT linear) with synthetic reserves
- **Graduation**: Migrates to PumpSwap (pump.fun's own AMM, NOT Raydium) — LP tokens burnt

- **Tax routing**: NO custom tax (flat 1% only)
- **Differentiation from AgentObservatory**: Solana (not Robinhood Chain); no AI agent layer; no stock pair integration; no ERC-6551; no MCP integration

### Virtuals Protocol (Base, $1.37B market cap)
- **Positioning**: "AI agent launchpad" — agent-as-asset (NOT agent-as-observer)
- **Differentiation from AgentObservatory**: Base (not Robinhood Chain); no stock pairs; agent-as-asset (collides with Virtuals if AgentObservatory mispositioned — that's why AgentObservatory uses "agent-as-observer" framing)

### Clanker (Base)
- **Ecosystem Fund**: Recycles protocol fees into CLANKER buybacks, grants, infra
- **Differentiation from AgentObservatory**: Base (not Robinhood Chain); no Robinhood Trading MCP integration; no stock pairs

### Robinhood in-app Agent Apps
- **Status**: Closed B2B-partner surface (11 curated data-source partners: Unusual Whales, Nasdaq, SpotGamma, etc.)
- **Differentiation from AgentObservatory**: Closed (not open); custodial (not non-custodial); in-app only (not cross-chain portable); data-source-only (not tokenized agent ownership)

- **Daily pair volume**: $443M in early October 2026 (up from near-zero in July 2026)
